Play together

Multiplayer

BeaverBuddies Stability Fork

Co-op with fewer crashes and desyncs.

An independent fork of BeaverBuddies for one shared colony, with Steam friend invites, a connection panel, teammate cursors and fixes for desyncs, crashes and reconnects. Feature-complete: it gets fixes and game updates, and new features go into MultiColony.

  • Invite Steam friends from Steam's overlay, no port forwarding
  • A connection panel with ping, sync state and chat
  • Refuses a mismatched build before it can desync

The newest release is fixes only and hasn't been played yet; the one before it was.

Multiplayer

BeaverBuddies MultiColony

Two players, one map, a colony each.

Built on the Stability Fork. Each player runs a colony of their own, with its own beavers, stock, science and working hours, on one shared map. The colonies' roads never join: they trade only at Trading Posts. Or share one colony, as in the fork.

  • A waiting room before a new game: invite, ready up, start together
  • Trading Posts exchange goods and beavers between colonies
  • Folktails and Iron Teeth can play in one game

Beta: the release candidate. Founding, building, Trading Posts and the waiting room were played; starting from the waiting room, mixed factions and the late game haven't been yet. Use a copy of a save.

Performance

Late Game Performance

Fewer lag spikes in big colonies.

Keeps answers the game has already worked out, moves heavy work such as route maps and save snapshots to other CPU cores, and skips lookups that can't change the result. Beavers decide exactly what they would anyway.

  • Built for BeaverBuddies co-op: the same code on every computer
  • Switches a feature off if anything unexpected happens
  • Timing lines in Player.log, to measure your own colony

Young mod: played in a few sessions in one large colony. Try it on a copy of a save.

Requires Harmony 2.4.1+, Mod Settings

Housing

Optimized Local Housing

Shorter commutes, solved once a day.

Once a day, moves adult beavers into the homes that give the whole colony the shortest total walk to work. Every home keeps its adults, and children never move.

  • Solves each district exactly, with the game's own route costs
  • Spread over many ticks, so there's no freeze
  • Turns itself off alongside Housing Optimize or Commute Balancer

Its first stable code was played; the newest changes are checked but not played yet.

Requires Nothing extra

Beaver needs

Hungry Pathing

Fed before the whistle, not after.

During working hours, beavers eat and drink before the hunger penalty, timed to their shift, at the closest stocked storage. Off duty nothing changes, and they don't eat more.

  • A three-hour buffer on Hunger and Thirst
  • Leaves a far job early enough to arrive with the buffer in hand
  • Nothing written to saves; built for co-op

Beta: try it on a copy of your save first.

Requires Harmony 2.4.1+

Storage

MixedStorage

Several goods in one warehouse.

Divide a warehouse or pile by percentage, say 50% carrots and 50% gears, with limits per good and a compact panel that shows exactly what is stored.

  • Split any warehouse or pile by percentage per good
  • Copy and paste an allocation between buildings
  • One download for single player and BeaverBuddies co-op

Planning

Persistent Work Areas

Keep working-area outlines on screen.

Pin a farm, forester or gatherer's working area and its outline stays after you deselect it. Pick a crop or tree and the buildings that plant it are outlined too.

  • Overlapping pins merge into one outline
  • Clear pinned areas in one click, or with a key
  • Display only: saves untouched, pins local to each player

Played extensively in game, planting-tool outlines and remembered pins included.

Building

The Tipsy Tail

A swim-up pool bar for your beavers.

A timber-and-thatch pool bar for eight beavers at once: a Social Life need of its own worth up to +2 well-being, plus Wet fur relief like the Lido. Haulers keep it filled with water.

  • A 5 × 6 site on solid ground, recessed into it
  • Runs on hauled water and stops when it runs dry
  • Folktails and Iron Teeth, under Well-being

Installing any of these

Every mod installs the same way. Each mod's own site has a longer guide with troubleshooting.

  1. 1

    Download the ZIP

    Use the download button under the mod. If you grab it from GitHub yourself, pick the ZIP under Assets, not Source code.

  2. 2

    Extract it into Mods

    With Timberborn closed, extract the mod's folder into Documents\Timberborn\Mods. Avoid an extra nested folder.

  3. 3

    Enable it in the game

    Start Timberborn, turn the mod on in the mod manager, and restart if prompted. Enable anything listed under Requires too.

Harmony and Mod Settings

These are separate mods from the Steam Workshop. Each mod above says which ones it needs.

Playing co-op

Every player installs the same version of the mod and runs the same game version.

Beta and preview builds

Builds marked Beta or Preview are early. Try them on a copy of a save, and report problems on the GitHub issues page.
